01 About

I work slowly,
on purpose.

Most of the web fights for your attention. I'm interested in the opposite: pages that do one thing, do it well, and get out of the way.

Right now that means rebuilding this portfolio, shipping small practice builds, and growing one real product — the Undistracted Timer.
